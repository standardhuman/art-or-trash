About This Experiment

We present you with images. Some hang in prestigious institutions where people pay admission to stare at them thoughtfully. Others were photographed moments before being collected by sanitation workers.

Your job is to determine which is which. Good luck with that.

The real question isn't whether you're correct—it's whether humanity can collectively distinguish between priceless cultural artifacts and actual refuse. Spoiler: the results are fascinating.
