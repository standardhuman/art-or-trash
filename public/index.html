Art or Trash?

Can you tell museum art from literal garbage?

How to Play

You'll see one image at a time - it could be priceless artwork from world-famous museums or actual photos of trash.

  1. Look carefully at the image
  2. Make your choice: Is it Art 🎨 or Trash 🗑️?
  3. Get instant feedback - were you right?
  4. Auto-advance to the next image after 3 seconds

Think you have a refined artistic eye? This game might surprise you!
