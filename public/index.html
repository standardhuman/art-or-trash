About Art or Not?

This game challenges you to distinguish between priceless masterpieces from world-renowned museums and absolute trash. The "art" comes from prestigious institutions like the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Rijksmuseum, and the Art Institute of Chicago—you know, the fancy stuff worth millions. The "not art" is pure garbage: literal dumpster finds, sidewalk filth, mysterious stains, forgotten leftovers, and other revolting refuse that somehow looks disturbingly artistic.

Warning: It's embarrassingly difficult! Those pretentious contemporary art pieces often look like hot garbage, while some actual rubbish appears shockingly museum-worthy. Think you're cultured? Think you know art from trash? Prove it!
