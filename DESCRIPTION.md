# Art or Trash? 🎨🗑️

## What is it?

Art or Trash is a fun, educational game that challenges your ability to distinguish between genuine museum artwork and literal garbage. It's the ultimate test of whether you can tell a masterpiece from a mess!

## How it works

1. **Simple Gameplay**: You're shown one image at a time - it could be a priceless artwork from world-famous museums or an actual photo of trash and garbage.

2. **Make Your Choice**: Click the Art button (or press 1) if you think it's a museum piece, or the Trash button (or press 2) if you think it's garbage.

3. **Instant Feedback**: The game immediately tells you if you're right or wrong, revealing details about the artwork (title, artist, museum) or confirming it was indeed trash.

4. **Auto-Advance**: After 3 seconds, the next image loads automatically, keeping the game flowing smoothly.

5. **Track Your Performance**: See global statistics showing how well everyone distinguishes art from trash. Are humans better at recognizing art or identifying garbage?

## Features

- **Real Museum Art**: Includes works from the Metropolitan Museum, Rijksmuseum, and Art Institute of Chicago
- **Actual Trash Photos**: Curated collection of garbage, waste, and refuse images
- **Keyboard Controls**: Play entirely with your keyboard for faster gameplay
- **History Navigation**: Go back to review previous images and learn about the art you missed
- **Submit Images**: Contribute your own art or trash finds to expand the collection
- **Mobile Friendly**: Works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops

## The Challenge

Think you have a refined artistic eye? This game might surprise you! Some modern art can look like random junk, while some garbage arrangements can appear surprisingly artistic. It's a humorous yet thought-provoking exploration of the age-old question: "What is art?"

Perfect for art students, museum lovers, or anyone who's ever looked at a piece of modern art and thought, "I could have made that!"
